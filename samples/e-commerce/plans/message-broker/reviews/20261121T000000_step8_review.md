### Step-1. Test 10.1 asserts `KafkaException`, but `send(...).get()` most likely throws `ExecutionException`

Quote: `assertThatThrownBy(() -> template.send(undeclared, KEY, new byte[0]).get()).isInstanceOf(KafkaException.class);`. Step: "10.1 ... THEN the publish fails AND no topic is created."
