### 1. Spring's `ETag` is not a drop-in for the core one

Consequence: reading `If-Match`, the 400 on a malformed value, the `W/"0"` value and what `CommandHandler` returns were guessed.
Belongs to: spec, plan
Resolution: fixed: `CommandHandler` and facades return a `long`; the controller writes `new ETag(String.valueOf(v), true).formattedTag()`, reads with `ETag.parse`; malformed throws `IllegalArgumentException` (400 as today).

### 2. Shop test helpers use the core `ETag`

Consequence: compile failures not named in the step.
Belongs to: plan
Resolution: fixed: step 5 names `ApiSpecification`, `PurchaseRestBuilder`, `RemoveProductItemFromShoppingCartTests`, `CheckOutShoppingCartTests`.

### 3. The conflict exception and `RetryException`

Consequence: a new exception type could break the 412 mapping; `execute` would surface a checked `RetryException` as 500.
Belongs to: spec, plan
Resolution: fixed: `ConcurrentModificationException` for `AppendResult.Conflict`, `RetryTemplate.invoke`.

### 4. Kafka `awaitCaughtUp` on `RetryTemplate` has no step

Consequence: the loop stays, or the deadline is per consumer group.
Belongs to: plan
Resolution: fixed: done by the coordinator in the CI change before its commit (recorded in step 3's "already done"); one deadline for the whole wait; `IllegalStateException` naming group and topic, rethrown by `invoke`. Covered by the bus tests that wait and step 3's 16.2 tests.

### 5. `receiveAll` on Awaitility changes its timeout behaviour

Consequence: callers asserting a short list would time out instead.
Belongs to: plan
Resolution: fixed: it waits for the expected number and fails on timeout; its callers assert that number.
