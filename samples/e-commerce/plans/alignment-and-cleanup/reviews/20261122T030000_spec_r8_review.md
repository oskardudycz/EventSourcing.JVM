### Quality-2. "Caught up" and chains of messages

Quote: "handled or dead-lettered every message published before the call"; "A test that proves something does not happen waits ... then asserts once."
Consequence: a flow test that waits once for a chain is flaky.
Belongs to: qa
Resolution: fixed in r9 without a new decision, following Q13 ("infra that allow waiting for the catch up") and the r8 rule that `atMost` stays as the limit of a wait on a condition: a test that waits for the end of a chain waits on the chain's last observable result (a projection row, a published message) with Awaitility `atMost`; the catch-up waits serve the "nothing more happens" checks of one bus or one subscription. On the in-memory store and bus (`ECommerceTests`) the whole chain runs before `publish` or `append` returns.
